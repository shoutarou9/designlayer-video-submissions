You are a motion designer. Following the spec below exactly, build a launch film rendered with the browser's Canvas 2D API, export the frames with Playwright, and encode them to MP4 with ffmpeg.

0. Render contract (must follow)
The film is a pure function of time. window.seek(t) draws frame t. Never carry state over from a previous frame.
In export mode, do not use CSS transitions, setTimeout, or requestAnimationFrame.
Randomness only from seeded mulberry32 (Math.random is forbidden). Wobble/jitter uses value noise (interpolated hashes).
Video-file assets are extracted to numbered image sequences with ffmpeg; pick the frame with floor((t - start) * fps).
Export: open a 1920×1080 page in Playwright, seek(f/60) → CDP Page.captureScreenshot → pipe the PNG into ffmpeg. Encoding settings: -c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 16 -pix_fmt yuv420p -vf scale=out_color_matrix=bt709.
Picture and sound timing both reference the same timeline.json (in beats). After producing the audio, measure the actual beat positions and pass them to the film as beats.js ({t0, period}).
Review procedure: before exporting the full film, export one frame per beat, assemble them into a contact sheet, inspect it visually, and fix what you see.
0.1 Workspace and environment (added)
Start in a new, empty folder. Do not open or reuse any existing project folder, even one with a similar name.
Required tools: Node.js (with Playwright + Chromium), ffmpeg/ffprobe. Python with numpy/scipy may be used for audio analysis.
Fonts: install Inter Variable and Instrument Serif locally (e.g. from npm @fontsource-variable/inter and @fontsource/instrument-serif) and load them with FontFace.
If assets/bg_nebula.webp is not supplied, generate it procedurally (seeded fBm noise, deep indigo → violet → lilac, sparse stars) at 1536×1024.

3. Shared look
Text entrance (focus reveal): 0.55 s quartOut — blur 16px → 0, horizontal slide of 12–40px, opacity 0 → 1. Exit: expoIn over 0.32 s, blurring out as it fades. Where specified, the color shifts from blue (#3D63F0) to white.
Aurora (light leak): four elliptical gradients along the bottom edge, stacked with 'lighter' compositing.
(95%, 104%) 980×430 #2F5BFF
(42%, 110%) 1150×300 lilac
(60%, 113%) 650×200 white
(-2%, 102%) 700×360 #3326A8
Positions drift slowly with value noise.
Film grain: four 256px noise tiles, switched at 24 fps, overlay blending, opacity 0.05.
Easings: quartOut / expoOut / expoIn / expoInOut / cubicInOut / backOut.
Logo drawing: measure the 5 paths with getTotalLength() and draw them one at a time using lineDash (stagger 0.08–0.12 s, 0.7–0.85 s per path, expoInOut).

5. Sound (all synthesized in code; 128 BPM, D major family)
Pad (the lead): one chord per bar — Dmaj9 Dmaj9 Bm9 Gmaj9 Em9 A6 Dmaj9 Bm9 Gmaj9 A6 Dmaj9 Bm9 Gmaj9 Em9. Five slow-attack sawtooth voices, softened with a lowpass filter.
Opening:
A pluck on each of the two words.
A key-press sound plus a small pluck every time the keycap changes.
On selection, a click and a short whoosh.
Pasting: a key press plus a short blip that rises in pitch with each paste. An upward whoosh for the scroll. A soft impact hit at the flash.
Haze: a low chord and a slow, heartbeat-like sub-bass. A bell when the focus lands.
Logo: a bell arpeggio.
Chat:
A key press per character.
Whooshes on camera push-in and pull-out.
A click on send and a sideways whoosh.
A glittering shimmer while searching; three pops as the results appear.
Tunnel: a restrained groove (kick, clap, hi-hat, bass). A small whoosh each time a ring passes. A riser and a short snare roll before the transition. An impact hit just before the transition.
Lockup: a bell, a key press for each letter, a chord of bells on MCP, low end plus a bell on the CTA.
Mix (keep it calm, not loud):
Levels relative to the pad: whoosh 0.4, impact hit 0.5, click 0.4, key press 0.45, pop 0.45, clap 0.4, hi-hat 0.35, kick 0.6, bass 0.7, chord stab 0.55, sub-bass 0.35, pluck 0.65, riser 0.6. No shaker.
Reverb 2.4 s, send 0.11. An 8.5 kHz lowpass on the master.
Finally, normalize with two-pass loudnorm to -18 LUFS / True Peak -1 dB.
